## Findings and what changed

   the start of a long line; `find_quote` required whole lines, marked the citation
   unverified and lowered the confidence from high to medium. A quote of at least 12
   characters may now cut a line short (first line a line's end, last line a line's
   start, a one-line quote any part of a line). Re-checked against ArchDrawer: all
   citations verified, confidence kept.

   comment footer now says "read-only, code not run" and the prompt asks for the
   specific open point; afterwards 1 of 23 backtest analyses still said it.
